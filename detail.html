WhatsApp Group Joining Rules & Safety Guidelines

To maintain a safe and spam-free community for everyone, please follow these simple rules before entering the group:

  • Respect All Members: Treat group admins and members with courtesy. No abusive language or hate speech.
  • No Unsolicited Spam: Avoid sending promotional links, affiliate schemes, or unnecessary forward messages.
  • Stay On-Topic: Ensure your messages align strictly with the group's specific category or topic.
  • Protect Personal Privacy: Do not share sensitive personal information, banking details, or OTPs inside public groups.
  • Report Dead Links: If this group is full, revoked, or inactive, return to the main directory page and click Report so our team can replace it.
